About Me

Hi, I'm Harriet Curran, a dedicated Dental Hygienist and Dental Therapist passionate about helping people achieve healthy, confident smiles. My journey combines a strong foundation in science with a creative background, allowing me to bring a unique, personable approach to my practice.

Qualifications

  • University of Leeds – Graduate Diploma in Dental Hygiene/Dental Restorative Therapy, 2017
  • BSc in Microbiology, 2010
  • BA (Hons) in Acting and Performing, 2007

Interests & Approach

I particularly enjoy paediatric care and restorative treatments. Working in a field that can be anxiety-inducing for many, I strive to create a positive, comforting experience for every patient. Helping people feel at ease and confident about their oral health is at the heart of what I do.
